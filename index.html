Why vertical drama is hard to automate

A 60-second vertical episode is not one prompt. It is a script broken into shots, a cast that must stay recognizable, dialogue that needs a voice, and captions that have to survive being watched on mute. Most text-to-video tools do one of those well and leave the rest to a human with a timeline.

Pavo AI approaches the problem from the script side. You paste a script or an outline, and the tool proposes a shot list: what the camera sees, who is in frame, and how long each beat should last. From there it generates the shots, keeps characters consistent with reference images, adds AI voice-over, burns in captions, and exports a 9:16 clip ready for Reels, TikTok or Shorts.

What the workflow looks like

  • Script to shot list. Scenes are split into individual shots with prompts you can edit before spending any generation time.
  • Character consistency. Reference images anchor a character's face, hair and wardrobe across shots, which is the difference between a series and a slideshow.
  • Voice and captions. AI voice-over in multiple languages, with captions styled for mobile and burned into the picture.
  • 9:16 by default. Frames are composed for a phone held upright, not cropped from a landscape render.

Who it is for

Short-drama studios shipping several episodes a week, solo creators who write serialized fiction, and social teams that need a steady stream of narrative clips without booking a cast, a location and a crew. The useful comparison is not "AI vs. a film set" — it is "a first cut tonight vs. a first cut next month".

If you want to see the current feature set and pricing, the product page is at pavoai.app. There is also an open-source MCP server that exposes the workflow to agents: pavo-ai-mcp.

Notes on quality

AI video still fails in recognizable ways: hands, crowds, fast camera moves, and dialogue-heavy scenes where lip sync matters. The practical approach is to keep shots short, prefer medium and close framing, and treat each shot as a separate generation you can retry. Pavo AI is designed around that reality — per-shot regeneration instead of re-rendering a whole scene.
